Privacy policy

Your marketplace data should have a clear purpose.

This policy explains the information EDGE uses during its public prelaunch alpha.

Effective: 11 August 2026

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication provides EDGE with your account identifier and basic profile information such as your name, email address, and profile image. EDGE also stores information you submit, including your role, biography, genres, location, portfolio references, gigs, applications, booking outcomes, and reviews.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, operate marketplace workflows, show relevant profiles and opportunities, prevent duplicate or unauthorized actions, calculate reliability indicators, provide support, and improve the alpha.

Service providers

EDGE uses Google Firebase for authentication, database storage, security rules, hosting support, and server-side functions. Google processes information under its applicable service terms and privacy commitments. EDGE does not sell personal information.

Visibility and sharing

Marketplace profile fields, gigs, applications, outcomes, and reviews may be visible to other signed-in users when required for the relevant workflow. Private authentication credentials are not displayed to marketplace users.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained while needed to operate the alpha, preserve booking and trust records, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact us from the email associated with your account.

Security and alpha limitations

EDGE uses Firebase Authentication and database access rules to restrict operations. No system is perfectly secure, and this prelaunch build should not be used for highly sensitive information.

Children

EDGE is not intended for children under 18. Do not use the service if you are not legally able to enter marketplace arrangements in your location.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as EDGE develops. Material changes will be reflected on this page. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to edge911info@gmail.com.
