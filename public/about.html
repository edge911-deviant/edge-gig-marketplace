EDGE — Gig Marketplace

EDGE — Gig Marketplace is a mobile-first marketplace for live-performance bookings, where artists discover gigs and organisers publish, review, hire, complete, and review performance opportunities.

Purpose of EDGE — Gig Marketplace

  • Artists can search open gigs, apply once, track decisions, and maintain a reliability record.
  • Organisers can publish opportunities, review applicants, hire performers, and record outcomes.
  • Both sides can leave post-booking reviews to improve marketplace trust.

Alpha status

This public build is an early alpha for product testing. Payments are not processed in the current release, and functionality may change as the marketplace is validated.

Google sign-in

EDGE uses Google sign-in through Firebase Authentication to establish an account securely. The app requests basic identity information needed to create and protect your marketplace profile.
